Report a post or an author

On any post that isn't yours, open its ⋯ menu, or press and hold the post, and choose Report. Pick the reason that fits. A moderator reviews reports within 24 hours, and the author isn't told who reported them.

To stop seeing someone's posts and replies, choose Block from the same menu. You can unblock them in Settings → Community → Blocked authors.

A hidden post or a suspension

If a moderator hid one of your posts or suspended your posting and you think that was a mistake, email us from the address on your account. Tell us which post it is, and we'll look at it again.

Delete your account

Email us from the address on your account and ask us to delete it. We delete your plans, saved trips, GPS tracks, posts, photos, follows and public name. A post that other climbers replied to stays as a placeholder with no name and no text, so their replies still make sense.
